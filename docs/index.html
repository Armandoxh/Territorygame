HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap a region

    Your units fill the region you tap. The pulsing white outline shows your active target.

  • Build to grow stronger

    Long-press your land or use the S / T / A hotbar.
    Settlement = +gold +troops · Turret = harder to take · Airstrip = unlocks bombs.

  • Manage your army

    Each tile you take costs gold and troops. Big armies attack faster and defend tougher — but expanding too quickly leaves you weak.

  • Win at 95%

    Control 95% of the map's land tiles to win. Your % shows in the top bar.
