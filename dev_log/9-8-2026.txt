Build 32 (v0.13.0): RUSH HOUR -- Arc 2 (strategy) begins. Deterministic
3-minute cycle; the last 45s are rush hour on one unlocked line
(rotating in ladder order), x2.5 demand at every station it serves.
The sim probe proved the strategic shape: at base capacity a rush pays
0.98x (queues cap, trains full -- nothing), cars L5 pays 1.37x, cars L5
+ extra train 1.91x. Rush hour is a dividend on pre-built capacity
headroom, making per-line investment positional for the first time.
rushClock persists (save v13) so the schedule continues across reloads;
offline time doesn't advance it (rushes reward presence). UI: a red
RUSH HOUR strip with line bullet + countdown while active, a quiet
NEXT RUSH countdown while calm; the rush line draws fatter on the map;
a low double-strike sound marks rush start. Thresholds re-measured with
rush active (access 1.04, trainset 1.03, marketing 1.02 -- demand-side
upgrades idle through saturated windows by design). New tests: schedule
timing, ladder-order rotation, capacity-pays-the-rush (>1.5x window
comparison), rushClock round-trip.

Build 37 (v0.17.0): THE LIGHT CYCLE (player: "I still want AAA

current stack before considering a Three.js renderer). The map now

blur only, all CanvasKit-safe. STYLE.md records the light-cycle law.
