2026-05-13 — production effects (nail #9)

The lynchpin nail. Up until now, buildings did nothing —
the menu let you tier up, the matching ★ was decoration.
Now each built tier outputs resources per second, and the
matching boost (2x) is real.

Currency layout (one per building line):
  settlement -> pop
  farmland   -> food
  forestry   -> wood
  mining     -> ore
  merchant   -> gold

Per-tier base rates (units/sec):
  tier 1 = 1   tier 2 = 3   tier 3 = 7

Match boost: 2x. Applied iff the line's matching resource
(see resources.matchingLineFor) equals the state's
resource. Settlement is universal (no matching resource by
design) — gets no boost, just always produces pop on any
state.

Implementation:
- economy.ts: Currency / Economy / productionRate /
  currencyForLine. Rate table lives here, two-constant
  tuning surface.
- nation.ts: Nation gains `economy: Economy`, init'd to
  all-zeros in spawnNations.
- main.ts: tickProduction(dtMs) per frame. Iterates alive
  nations × owned states × lines, adds rate * dtSec to
  nation.economy[currency]. ~750 ops/tick. Cheap.
- Readout: one new line shows the player's five totals as
  floor()'d integers so the eye isn't tracking fractional
  per-frame deltas.

What this nail does NOT do (deferred — separate nails):
- Costs: buildings still free + instant. economy only
  accumulates. No drain side.
- Spending: no army upgrades, no consumable, no victory
  by economy. Resources are write-only for now.
- Cap: numbers grow unbounded. Will probably gate pop
  growth on food when both costs land.
- AI economy: AIs don't build (the build menu is
  player-only), so AI economies stay at 0. The gap
  widens until "AI builds too" lands — which now has
  a meaningful payoff to wire up since production
  exists.

Loop the player can now see:
1. Tap region in own territory -> snap-zoom in.
2. Tap a state -> menu (matching ★ tab auto-active).
3. Build tier 1 of the matching line.
4. Watch the corresponding currency tick up in the
   readout.
5. Build tier 2, tier 3 — rate doubles, quadruples.
6. Build the matching line on non-matching states for
   half the income (1x not 2x).

The "where to invade next" question (locked-line rule
shipped last nail) now has a quantitative answer in the
readout: if your wood is climbing slowly you need more
forest states.

Open hooks for the next nail:
- Build costs land in stateMenu (gate the available->
  tap-to-build transition on having enough of a
  currency). The buildTier validator should also
  consume from nation.economy.
- AI building: AI picks states + lines, calls buildTier
  on its own behalf. Bias toward matching lines and
  tiering up over building everywhere — keeps the
  AI strategically focused.
- Spending sinks: army recruitment cost (currently free
  too) could draw from gold or pop. Wires neatly into
  the existing getRecruitmentRate path.
