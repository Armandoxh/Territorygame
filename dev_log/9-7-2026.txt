Metro Magnate build 21 (v0.8.1): live-fleet train placement (player:
"spacing feels solely dependent on when I click... place them
bidirectionally equidistant"). Correct: van der Corput slots only
described spawn-time positions and ignored where the fleet had MOVED by
purchase time. _spawnTrain now maps each existing train to its circular
round-trip phase (0..2L; <L = heading up at distance p, >=L = heading
back) and spawns the new train at the midpoint of the WIDEST phase gap:
a 2nd train enters exactly half a round trip from the 1st wherever it
is (same track, opposite direction); every later purchase drops into
the biggest hole. Same line speed for all trains means the spacing
persists. At t=0 the scheme reproduces the old vdc pattern exactly, so
the existing spawn test still pins it; a new test buys at staggered odd
moments (13.7s, +23.3s, +8.9s) and asserts the diametric entry and a
quarter-round-trip minimum pairwise gap.

Build 22: build 21's Sim run flagged the OLD spawn test, not the engine
-- with 4 equal phase gaps the 5th train's slot is a tie, and a 1-ulp
float difference picked the L/4-heading-DOWN slot over L/4-heading-up
(same perfect spacing, mirrored). The legacy test asserted the exact
vdc-era pattern; rewrote it to pin the BEHAVIOR: exact minimum circular
phase gap after each back-to-back purchase (L, L/2, L/2, L/4) plus the
deterministic 2nd-train-opposite entry. Lesson: assert invariants, not
incidental tiebreaks.
