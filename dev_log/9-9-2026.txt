Build 44 (metro2): iOS ZOOM-TRAP FIX (player: stations near another
line hard to click; double-tapping them "makes the whole screen zoom
in (buttons too) and I can't fix it without refresh"). Root cause:
iOS Safari IGNORES user-scalable=no for double-tap and pinch PAGE
zoom -- rapid re-taps on a fiddly station triggered browser zoom of
the whole page, and the same flag then blocks pinching back out.
Fix, three layers: (1) touch-action: manipulation on the page
(the modern, honored way to disable double-tap zoom; the scrolling
panel gets pan-y + overscroll-behavior: contain, the canvas keeps
none), (2) gesturestart/change/end + dblclick preventDefault, (3) a
320ms double-tap suppressor scoped to the CANVAS ONLY so buy buttons
keep fast repeated taps (canvas input is pointer-event driven, so no
click is lost). Also the trigger itself: station pick tolerance
raised (min 4->5.5 world units, zoom scale 0.02->0.028) so stops
sitting against a neighboring line's ribbon are comfortably
tappable. 26 tests green; headless regression re-ran the buy +
persistence flow with the guards active.
