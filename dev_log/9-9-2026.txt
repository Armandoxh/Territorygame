Build 55 (metro2): FLUID TRAINS (player: "when the train moves it
doesn't feel very fluid, almost pixelated"). Root cause: the sim
ticks at a fixed 20Hz (0.05s, for determinism) and trains rendered
only at those snapshots -- 20 positions/sec on a 60-120Hz phone =
visible stepping. Standard fixed-timestep interpolation added: the
frame loop snapshots train distances before each tick and render()
blends prev->current by alpha = acc/step, so motion is continuous at
any refresh rate while the sim stays bit-identical (28 tests green,
untouched). Probe confirmed interpolated positions BETWEEN tick
values gliding at exactly line speed; the first probe's all-zero
deltas were a station dwell, not a bug.
