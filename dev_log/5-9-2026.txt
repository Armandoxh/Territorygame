[pending] gameplay: per-vassal army autonomy (defend > expand) for
HUMANS + AIs

WHAT: Replaced _aiCommandArmies (AI-only, brute "march all
armies on closest enemy capital") with _vassalCommandArmies
which runs for every alive player. Per region a player
dominates (their "vassal"):
  1. DEFENSE — any enemy army inside the vassal region:
     dispatch the local non-manual armies at the closest
     enemy stack.
  2. EXPANSION — no threat in region: send local armies to
     the nearest enemy/neutral tile bordering the region.
     War-enemies preferred over neutrals so vassals press
     active wars.
  3. FOREIGN PUSH — armies that wandered into a non-vassal
     region (paratrooper drop, blitz spawn near the front,
     mid-conquest stack) push toward the closest hostile
     tile in that region instead of stalling.

Manual orders honored — armies with manual=true (set via
setArmyTarget on player tap) are skipped entirely. When a
manual march completes, manual flag clears and the vassal
picks the army back up. Throttled to ~3s per player, staggered.

WHY: User: "When I drop armies and they take over a vassal it
should go to that vassals power. If a vassal needs to defend
against enemy armies it should dispatch armies to fight them.
If a vassal needs to expand it should send its armies to grab
either enemy or neutral land."

The previous AI logic only fired for non-humans and only
targeted enemy capitals; it ignored local threats and didn't
exist at all for the human player. Vassals were inert: a
player's barracks would auto-recruit armies that just sat on
the spawn tile until manually tapped. Now every vassal
region runs its own little brain — captured tiles inside
the vassal accrue to that vassal's regional dominance, and
the vassal's local armies actively defend / expand without
human micromanagement.
