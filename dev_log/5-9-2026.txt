     ARMY_AURA_RADIUS=10, up to 3 neutral + 1 enemy claim per

[a51395f] gameplay: aura BFS-foothold so claims actually radiate
from the army (not the empire's whole frontier)

WHAT: Previous aura scanned a 21×21 box and claimed any non-owner
tile that touched ANY of the player's tiles. For an army inside
a big empire, that bubble overlapped a long stretch of the
existing empire frontier — claims appeared all along that
frontier, looking like the legacy flat flood.

Replaced the box-scan with BFS from the army's tile through
OWNED tiles, bounded to ARMY_AURA_RADIUS=10 cardinal hops.
Foothold = owned tiles reachable from army within R hops.
Candidates = non-owner cardinal neighbors of any foothold tile.

Result:
  - Paratrooper on neutral land → foothold-step claims drop tile
    → BFS finds candidates at 4 cardinal sides → web grows
    concentrically from the drop point.
  - Army deep in own empire → BFS fills R hops, no non-owner
    neighbors → candidates empty → no aura claims (army is
    "settled"; user moves it elsewhere). Strength still regens.
  - Army at the empire's edge → BFS fills locally → claims only
    along the edge near the army, not 30 tiles away on the other
    side of the empire.

WHY: User: "It's not expanding to that land it's attacking, it's
more like a flat expansion of my territory across the board. Do
the expansion around that army itself."

Cost: BFS bounded by R=10 ≈ 100-300 visited tiles per army-tick.
At 20 armies total, ~5000 ops/tick — well within budget at 10Hz.

[f147be6] ux: persistent mode bar — always shows what your next
tap will do

WHAT: Replaced the old #place-banner (which only appeared during
build/bomb/ship modes) with #mode-bar — always visible, full
state machine, with a big visible ✕ that drops every active
mode + selection in one tap. Reads in priority order:
  default — "Tap an army · Long-press your land to build"
  bomb    — "💣 SMALL BOMB — tap target tile" (red)
  op      — "🚀 BLITZKRIEG — tap enemy land" (orange)
  ship    — "⚓ WARSHIP — tap a coastal tile" (blue)
  place   — "🏗 PLACING SETTLEMENT — tap your land" (gold)
  army    — "⚔️ ARMY ×123 — tap destination" (green)
  ship sel— "🚢 WARSHIP ×80 HP — tap destination" (green)

Selection mirrors:
  - HUD tracks selectedArmyId / selectedShipId.
  - main.ts calls hud.setArmySelection / setShipSelection on
    every selection change (select / switch / deselect / order).
  - Stale ids (unit died) auto-clear inside _refreshModeBar.
  - Strength / HP updates each frame so "ARMY ×123" is live.

Cancel pipe: hud.onCancelRequested wired in main.ts clears
renderer-side selection (HUD never reaches the renderer directly).
cancelAllModes() drops every mode + selection + fires "Cancelled"
toast. Toasts pruned — "Army selected" / "deselected" were
redundant once the banner shows persistent state.

WHY: User: "The controls are very non intuitive and it isn't
clear what needs to be done. Can you overhaul the controls and
make it obvious what's being clicked." Picked moments-of-
confusion: tap-canvas-does-nothing AND select-and-order-feels-
janky. Persistent banner addresses both — every tap now has an
in-context expectation ("this tap will...") shown on screen,
not after-the-fact toast.
