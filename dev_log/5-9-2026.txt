[eb0c5f3] gameplay: armies flood from drop location, drain →
regen → resume cycle (REVISION)

WHAT: Big revision after user playtested the first vassal-AI
cut and pushed back: armies should sit where dropped and web
outward like the legacy flood, not march out toward borders.

Changes:
  1. _armyAura rewritten: flood-fill from the army's CURRENT
     TILE (not bound to region). Webs out within
     ARMY_AURA_RADIUS=10, up to 3 neutral + 1 enemy claim per
     tick. Establishes a foothold by claiming the army's own
     tile first (so paratrooper drops on neutral / enemy land
     have a seed for the web). Only fires when army is idle
     (no destination).
  2. Drain → regen → resume cycle via per-army `recovering`
     flag. Drains to ARMY_AURA_EXHAUST_AT (=1) → recovering.
     Sleeps until strength climbs back to ARMY_AURA_RESUME_AT
     (=80) → resume. Aura drain never kills the army — only
     stack-vs-stack combat does.
  3. _vassalCommandArmies stripped to defense ONLY. Previous
     "push to nearest border" expansion path conflicted with
     the sit-and-flood model. Now: only redirect non-manual
     armies if an enemy stack is INSIDE one of the player's
     dominant regions.
  4. Bottom-roster nation badge: primary number changed from
     troop count ("823") to offense / defense multipliers
     ("1.5/1.2") — sourced from combatSummaryFor. Spy-network
     gate dropped (mults are decree fingerprints, not
     sensitive intel). Tile count "32t" still secondary.

WHY: User: "When an army is dropped on a neutral land they
should expand from where the army is until the army gets to 0.
Then the army should wait until it's at 80. Then auto expand
till zero. It should do the flat type of expansion we had
before where it webs out. From the army location. The number
that shows next to the country name should show their stats
like this offense/defense not '823g' nobody needs that info."

Combined: drop army → claim tile + web 10 tiles → drain to 1
→ park ~8s for regen → repeat. Manual tap-march overrides.
Defense AI still pulls armies off the flood when an invader
walks into the region.

[f147be6] ux: persistent mode bar — always shows what your next
tap will do

WHAT: Replaced the old #place-banner (which only appeared during
build/bomb/ship modes) with #mode-bar — always visible, full
state machine, with a big visible ✕ that drops every active
mode + selection in one tap. Reads in priority order:
  default — "Tap an army · Long-press your land to build"
  bomb    — "💣 SMALL BOMB — tap target tile" (red)
  op      — "🚀 BLITZKRIEG — tap enemy land" (orange)
  ship    — "⚓ WARSHIP — tap a coastal tile" (blue)
  place   — "🏗 PLACING SETTLEMENT — tap your land" (gold)
  army    — "⚔️ ARMY ×123 — tap destination" (green)
  ship sel— "🚢 WARSHIP ×80 HP — tap destination" (green)

Selection mirrors:
  - HUD tracks selectedArmyId / selectedShipId.
  - main.ts calls hud.setArmySelection / setShipSelection on
    every selection change (select / switch / deselect / order).
  - Stale ids (unit died) auto-clear inside _refreshModeBar.
  - Strength / HP updates each frame so "ARMY ×123" is live.

Cancel pipe: hud.onCancelRequested wired in main.ts clears
renderer-side selection (HUD never reaches the renderer directly).
cancelAllModes() drops every mode + selection + fires "Cancelled"
toast. Toasts pruned — "Army selected" / "deselected" were
redundant once the banner shows persistent state.

WHY: User: "The controls are very non intuitive and it isn't
clear what needs to be done. Can you overhaul the controls and
make it obvious what's being clicked." Picked moments-of-
confusion: tap-canvas-does-nothing AND select-and-order-feels-
janky. Persistent banner addresses both — every tap now has an
in-context expectation ("this tap will...") shown on screen,
not after-the-fact toast.
