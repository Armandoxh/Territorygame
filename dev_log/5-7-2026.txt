SESSION: 2026-05-07
=====================

Big mechanical shift session. Game went from "free-for-all expansion
with light diplomacy" to "Civ-style political model with peace as
default + commander-tree depth that actually applies in play".

Order is roughly chronological. Each entry is WHAT changed + WHY.

---

[a133e01] gameplay: manual click = 100% focus; vassals aggressive on
neutrals

WHAT: Restructured _expand candidate selection. Three modes per
frontier tile: (1) MANUAL — pool restricted to candidates inside a
manual-target region, no fallback; (2) VASSAL AUTONOMOUS — vassal
target first, else any adjacent neutral, with +50% rate boost when
targeting neutrals; (3) IDLE — non-vassal tile with no manual order
does nothing. Peace-state filter folded into candidate list.

WHY: User reported clicking a region "feels like spread 3× in all
directions" instead of focused push. Root cause: focus multiplier
applied to every leader-driven frontier tile regardless of whether it
could actually contribute to the front. Tiles with no target-region
neighbor fell through Tier 2/3 fallback and still got the boost,
attacking arbitrary unclaimed land at 3×. Fix makes "click = general's
order" literally true — only contributing tiles push, others sit out.

Also user wanted vassals to "expand autonomously and aggressively into
contacting neutrals" early game. The +50% boost in autonomous-on-
neutral mode addresses that.

---

[f5a6e5f] fix: diplomacy button vanished — bad position override on
#diplo-btn

WHAT: Removed `#diplo-btn { position: relative; }` rule.

WHY: Two commits earlier, that rule was added to anchor the new
notification badge. But .icon-btn already has position:absolute, and
overriding it with position:relative collapsed the button into normal
document flow — invisible behind the canvas. Absolute children resolve
against the nearest positioned ancestor, which the absolute parent
already provided.

[5a4fac7] ux: diplomacy panel defaults to active relationships only

WHAT: Hide "OTHER NATIONS" section by default; show only YOUR ALLIES
and AT WAR. "Show N Other Nations" toggle at the bottom expands to
the full list.

WHY: User wanted a panel that surfaces what they actually need to
manage. Listing 50 peaceful AIs was noise. Long-press enemy land
already proposes alliance, so propose-from-panel is rare; the
collapsed section preserves the option without making it the default.

[09a84b8] ux: diplomacy panel sections + war-invite validation + badge

WHAT: Three things. (1) Diplomacy panel grouped into sections:
ALLIES (green border), AT WAR (red border), OTHERS — instead of one
flat list. (2) War-invite popup drops stale invites if inviter is no
longer an ally. breakAlliance also clears any pending invites between

WHY: User reported (a) panel was overwhelming "AI 1, AI 2... AI 50"
with no order, (b) AI was sending war invites despite not being an
ally — turned out to be stale invites from broken alliances, and (c)
needed a way to know there's something to look at without opening
the panel.

[9b53f29] diplomacy overhaul: peace by default, permanent alliances,
war system

WHAT: Big one.
  • Default state for any pair = PEACE. tryCapture refuses to flip
    a defender's tile unless at war (or neutral). Same gate added
    to ship fire / submarine missiles / warship landings.
  • Aggression auto-declares war: tap an enemy region, drop a bomb
    on enemy land, or attack via ship. Toast shows cause→effect.
  • Alliances now permanent (Infinity expiry) instead of 60s.
    Forming an alliance ends any active war between the pair.
  • declareWar bandwagons the target's allies into the war on
    target's side (one level deep).
  • New diplomacy buttons: Declare War / Sue for Peace, Make Them
    Fight (coerce ally to declare war on your enemies).
  • coerceAllyToWar accept rule keys off ally vs target strength
    ratio; Embassy decree loosens the threshold.
  • War-invite system: AI declares war → if it has human ally,
    pendingWarInvites queue gets an entry. Red modal at top of
    screen with [Join War] / [Decline].
  • Ally indicator on map: capital crowns now color-coded —
    green double-halo for allies, red ring for enemies, white pulse
    for neutral.
  • _aiConsiderWar runs every ~10s per AI. Peaceful AIs prefer
    expanding into neutral. Only declare war when no neutrals
    adjacent AND 1.3× strength advantage.

WHY: User wanted a Civ-style political model: nations don't auto-
fight, aggression is a deliberate choice with diplomatic cost,
allies stick around long enough to matter, and "you can coerce
allies + AI invites you to wars". Game previously had alliances
existing but expiring after 60s and constant chaotic warfare with
no peace state. The map gave no visual indicator of who was allied.

[4818188] fix: AI players actually buy decrees now (full audit pass)

WHAT: New _aiBuyDecrees runs every ~10s per AI. Picks highest-priority
affordable decree from a mastery-tilted list (naval AIs lean into
admiralty/embassy/cartel, ground into iron-doctrine/bunkers/veterans,
air into air-supremacy/forced-march). Soft-cap 8 stacks per decree.

Audited every decree's effect path; documented in commit message.

WHY: User asked to verify all commander perks apply. Audit showed
every effect was wired — but buyDecree was only called from the human
HUD. AIs never invested in any decree, so their treasury just stockpiled
unused while the human stacked +30 forced-march unopposed. Asymmetric
late-game advantage hardcoded into the engine.

[b974df4] balance + perks: trade rebalanced, two new branches, four
new decrees

WHAT:
  • Trade per-region rate dropped 5g/s → 1.5g/s.
  • Diminishing returns on alliance count: 1st 100% / 2nd 70% /
    3rd 50% / 4th 35% / 5+ 25%.
  • Hard cap removed — diminishing returns naturally bound it.
  • New NAVAL branch: Admiralty (T1, +20% ship speed/–20% cost),
    Privateer (T2, coming soon).
  • New DIPLOMACY branch: Embassy (T1, AI more lenient), Cartel
    (T2, +20% trade income), Cold War (T3, coming soon).
  • Existing branches filled: Master Builder (econ), Reinforced
    Bunkers (def), Veterans (mil), Forced Labor (esp, coming soon).

WHY: User reported trade income compounded with commander boosts felt
"broken" — three alliances printing 120g/s. Per-region rate was 5g/s
with no diminishing, scaled linearly with allies. Lower base + dim
returns keeps trade meaningful (one good alliance = ~10g/s) without
snowballing. The two new branches address two earlier complaints —
"naval class doesn't get much love" and "trade alliances are central
now but have no decree tree".

KEY INVARIANTS LEARNED THIS SESSION
====================================

1. ALL tile-flip paths must destroy buildings. _claim does this
   centrally now — don't call territory.claim directly.
2. Decree effects must apply on BOTH manual and vassal paths. Helpers
   like _expansionBoostFor / _productionMult are called multiple
   places; always grep before assuming a new decree "works".
3. AI must invest in decrees (_aiBuyDecrees). Without that, any new
   decree is a one-sided human-only buff.
4. Peace is the default. tryCapture / ship fire / submarine missiles
   all gated on areAtWar(). Filter candidate lists upfront in _expand
   so we don't waste rolls.
5. Alliances are permanent (Infinity expiry) unless explicitly time-
   limited. allianceExpireAt() returning Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY is
   normal; HUD displays "ALLY" without countdown in that case.
6. Manual target click = "general's order" — only frontier tiles
   that can directly contribute push. Don't add Tier 2/3 fallback
   that bypasses the focus.
7. Tile-flip paths route through _claim, but pendingWarInvites and
   trade routes can become stale if alliances end — clean them up
   in breakAlliance.

[pending] gameplay: AC-130 buffed; new Stealth Bomber (1000g carpet)

WHAT:
  AC-130 buff:
    - Orbit duration 100 → 150 ticks (10s → 15s on station)
    - Strafe interval 14 → 9 ticks (~1.4s → ~0.9s, more passes)
    - Per-strafe blast radius 2 → 3
    - Per-strafe troop drain 25 → 60 (×2.4)
    - Per-strafe ship damage 15 → 35
    - Orbit-zone radius (BOMB_RADII['ac130']) 4 → 6 (wider area covered)
    Net: ~16 strafes per orbit covering a 12-tile-diameter footprint —
    cumulative wipe now solidly out-clears a Large bomb, justifying 350g.

  Stealth bomber:
    - New BombType 'stealth', cost 1000g, cooldown 60s
    - Plane speed 0.55 (fastest of any bomber)
    - On arrival, lays a CARPET of 7 bomblets along the flight axis
      from origin → dest, spaced 5 tiles apart, each radius 4
    - Net coverage ≈ 30 tiles long × 8 tiles wide
    - Per-bomblet 40 troop drain on owned tiles, 80 ship damage
    - Bypasses AA entirely — no rolledAA check applied
    - HUD: new bomb-sheet button with chevron icon, hotkey X

WHY:
  User said AC-130 felt underwhelming for its 350g cost. Per-strafe was
  too small (radius 2, 25 troops) and orbit was short, so total per-cast
  damage trailed Large bomb at 240g. Boosted across all axes.
  
  User asked for a Stealth Bomber: high-cost premium aircraft that does
  carpet bombing. Carpet implemented as a strip of overlapping bomblets
  along the flight axis (looks/feels like a bombing run). AA bypass is
  what justifies the 1000g — defenders can't stop it once launched, so
  it's the answer to a heavily-fortified AA umbrella.

[pending] gameplay: AA tiers + Defense Port + Artillery + ground perks

Late-game air dominance was a real problem — mass-bomb spam was the
dominant strategy and most AIs never built AA (it was air-mastery
gated). Ground play also lacked active push abilities while air/naval
had bombs/ships to tilt fights.

WHAT:
  AA overhaul:
    • Per-level hit rates: L1 50%, L2 60%, L3 75%, L4+ (consolidated)
      85%. _aaHitChanceFor helper.
    • AA included in _tryConsolidate so 5 same-tier cluster auto-
      upgrades (Bronze/Silver/Diamond), like settlements/turrets.
    • AA no longer gated on air-mastery — every player can build it.
      Defense should be universal; mastery still differentiates
      offense (airstrips/bombs).
    • aaCount per region cap added (1 per 50 tiles).

  Defense Port (NEW building):
    • New BuildingType 'port'. Cost 180g. Coastal-only (rejected if
      no adjacent water tile).
    • _portsTick scans every tick: each port rolls one shot per
      enemy ship in PORT_RADIUS (8). Hit rate uses same tier scale
      as AA (L1 50% → L4+ 85%). Damage 25 base, ×1.25 / ×1.50 / ×2
      by tier.
    • Same auto-consolidation as AA.
    • _anyEnemyHasShips drives AI port-build trigger (mirror of AA).
    • Build-sheet button hotkey P, anchor icon, blue-tinted ring.

  Artillery (NEW building):
    • New BuildingType 'artillery'. Cost 140g. Long range (turret
      base radius +3) but NO defense bonus — only retaliation.
      3× turret retaliation per level, so a bronze artillery covers
      a wide area and shreds attackers passing through.
    • Same auto-consolidation as turret.
    • Hotkey R. Cannon icon, orange-tinted ring.

  Ground active abilities (NEW):
    • Blitzkrieg (cost 900, 30s cooldown, instant): each region you
      dominate fires a BFS burst from its frontier, claiming up to
      10 tiles deep. Ignores defense. Only takes neutral or at-war
      tiles (no auto-declare). Per-region cap so the burst feels
      like a deep push from each vassal rather than a global cone.
    • Tank Rush (cost 1100, 80s cooldown, 15s duration): empire-
      wide ×2.0 attacker combat-power multiplier. Stacks
      multiplicatively with Veterans / Rally / focus.
    • Both wired into activateAbility with effects in _fireBlitzkrieg
      / _tankRushMult. Surface in the existing commander abilities
      panel automatically.

WHY: User reported (a) air-spam dominated late game with no real
counter, (b) AI rarely built AA so air players had free reign,
(c) ground mastery felt purely passive while air/naval had active
push tools. Three new structures + two new active abilities give
ground a comparable late-game power curve and force air players
to commit to bigger payloads (or stealth) to break through.

User asked for tank "steamroll" — implemented as Tank Rush ability
since per-tile units would require a lot more engine work.
Blitzkrieg is the per-vassal burst as requested. Both are listed
in the abilities panel and pay from treasury.

[pending] gameplay: Phase 3 — buildings cost resources + upkeep

WHAT:
  • New BUILDING_RESOURCE_COSTS table per BuildingType:
      settlement: 25 food + 15 wood
      turret:     30 stone + 15 wood
      airstrip:   40 stone + 25 oil + 5 gems
      aa:         25 stone + 15 oil
      port:       35 wood + 25 stone
      barracks:   30 wood + 20 stone
    Charged on tryBuild in addition to gold. Empire-wide pool (no
    per-vassal resource pool yet).
  • New BUILDING_UPKEEP table — per-tick resource drain per owned
    building. Settlement 0.002 food/tick, airstrip 0.005 oil/tick,
    AA 0.0015 stone + 0.001 oil/tick, etc.
  • _payBuildingUpkeep runs each tick. If owner can pay all components,
    drain. If not → mark Building.starvingSinceTick. After
    STARVING_DECAY_TICKS (30s) of continuous starvation the building
    self-destructs via _destroyBuildingsAt.
  • New BuildError 'resources' returned from tryBuild when any
    component is short. HUD toasts "Need more resources (see top bar)".
  • Build sheet now shows resource costs alongside gold cost
    ("60g 25🌾 15🌲"). Buttons disable when ANY component (gold OR
    resources) is unaffordable.

WHY: Phase 3 of the resource overhaul. User wanted buildings to cost
"a combination of resources" with upkeep. Forces the player to think
strategically about which regions to capture (your ability to build
turrets depends on owning stone-producing regions; airstrips need oil;
etc.). Sustained shortages start eating your infrastructure — late-
game neglect actually has consequences.

NEXT (Phase 4): trade overhaul — resource-specific trades, indefinite
flow until cancelled, AI-aware ("I need stone, propose stone-for-oil
trade"), with diplomatic pressure when needs aren't met.

[pending] gameplay: empire-wide deep defense — kills the
"snake" attack pattern through interior regions

WHAT: Added an empire-wide turret-investment bonus to _defenseAt
that activates when an attacker is fighting a defender's frontier
tile WITHOUT a turret in that specific region. Bonus scales with
total turret levels owned by the defender (0.4 per level, capped
at +2.5). Cached in _turretCountByOwner per build/destroy.

So: 5 turrets on the wall → +2.0 multiplier on every interior
frontier tile of yours; 8+ turrets → capped at +2.5.

takes over line by line like a snake." Diagnosis: an enemy
salient (created by a successful blitzkrieg or grinding push)

turrets STILL gets meaningful resistance in their interior
once a salient breaks through, without diluting the strong
in-region turret bonus. Wall investment now translates to
empire-wide resistance; the snake gets a 3× cost-per-claim
penalty on every roll instead of free progress.
