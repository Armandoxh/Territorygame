SESSION: 2026-05-08
=====================

Big mobile-UX session. User playtesting on phone surfaced a long
list of overlap / cutoff / missing-feature bugs; the second half
shifted toward gameplay tuning (team spawning, decree balance,
intel UI).

Order: chronological, each a single commit + WHY.

---

[a0ddd6b] ux: long-press enemy / ally land → Nation Profile sheet

WHAT: New modal triggered by long-press on any enemy or ally tile.
At-a-glance intel about that nation: tiles + % map, troops (gated
behind Spy Network), gold + treasury, building counts, resource
stockpile, trade flow with you + their net flow, plus context-
sensitive action buttons (trade / ally / war / peace). Replaces
old "long-press = trade prompt" — trade is now the top button,
so the gesture's muscle memory still works.

WHY: User asked "long press on a territory (enemy or ally) gives
stats about that nation, like exports, imports, military strength,
# of settlements, etc."

---

[bf0a256] fix: nation-sheet close button + mobile resource bar overflow

WHAT: (1) Trade-offer modal close button wasn't reliably firing
on mobile because _refreshNationSheet ran every frame and called
innerHTML='' on actions, destroying buttons mid-tap. Fixed by
gating button rebuild on a relationship-state sig — body text
still refreshes live, but action buttons only rebuild when
allied/atWar/teammate/route changes. Plus bumped the X close
button to 36×36 with -webkit-tap-highlight-color. (2) Resource
bar overflowed on narrow mobile (5 chips × labels didn't fit a
360px phone) — added a @media (max-width: 540px) block that
drops resource labels (color encodes them anyway), shrinks fonts.

WHY: User: "Exit out of the long press window isn't working but
otherwise it looks great on mobile. Also in mobile my resource
hud is cluttered and cut off."

[8a8d35d] graphics: bundle Noto Color Emoji subset for resource icons

WHAT: Added a Google Fonts subset of Noto Color Emoji with just

[ad561bf] gameplay: bigger team spawn clusters + non-overlapping team footprints

WHAT: Two sizing bugs in the team spawn picker. (1) clusterR
multiplier sqrt(N)*0.9 was too small — for teamSize=8 the disk
came out ~50 tiles, but packing 8 members at 20-tile minSep
needs ~56. Result: members landed on top of each other.
Bumped to sqrt(N)*1.5. (2) Inter-team separation could shrink
to half the cluster width when the loop fell through to lower
factors — teams' cluster footprints could overlap. Now floored
at 2.5× clusterR regardless of candidate-density formula.

WHY: User screenshot of an 8-man game showed all nation labels
crammed in one tight area with half the map empty.

[6557b2e] ux: distinguish you from teammates — wider hue spread + gold halo

WHAT: (1) Wider intra-team palette spread. Was ±4° hue + small
light/sat nudge per member (8-man teams looked like 8 nearly-
identical reds). Now ±18° total hue spread (clamped via
min(36°, teamSize×6)) plus a zig-zag light/sat pattern so
adjacent member indices look unmistakably different. Team
identity (red/blue/etc.) still reads. (2) Distinct GOLD halo
on your own capital — strong gold pulse ring (thicker), double
outer halo at 2.10× and 2.55× radius, bolder white outline on
the diamond body itself. Find yourself instantly even on a
crowded team-color map.

WHY: User: "at the start, hard to find which unit is mine
because they are all the same color."

[06a7783] balance: decree stacking curve — slow start, sharp ramp

WHAT: Switched main bonus decrees from constant-rate compound
(1+r)^N to a PROGRESSIVE formula where each stack k
contributes (rate × k)%, then multiplies. Slow start, sharp
accelerating ramp.
  mul = product over k=1..stacks of (1 + rate * k)
Per-decree rates retuned: production 0.015, forced-march
0.025/0.05 branch, veterans 0.012/0.025, bunkers 0.04/0.06/0.08,
standing-army 0.04, admiralty 0.03, cartel 0.03. Cost-reduction
decrees (master-builder, admiralty cost) keep constant-rate
compound — they have natural floors that bound the discount.
UI helpers (decreeCompactCurrent, decreeEffectFor) updated to
match. Engine cartel income also moved to progressive.

WHY: User: "upgrades have to be weaker at the jump, like
instead of 10% start at 1%. Since it compounds it'll scale
harder than it feels now." Stack-1 buys feel like investments
now, not "+20% all in" cliffs; combined with the geometric
cost ramp, going deep becomes a real commitment with sharper
late-stack payoff.

[pending] gameplay: snake fix v2 — defender troop fraction floor
+ stronger deep-defense bonus

WHAT: Two changes to the regional combat math.
  1. Defender's regional troop fraction now floors at 0.30
     (was raw `regionTiles / empireTiles`). For a 2000-tile
     empire defending a 100-tile region, the old math gave 5%
     of the empire's troops as local defense — a 1-tile enemy
     salient could attack with FULL empire power vs 5% defender
     and grind unopposed. With a 30% floor, defenders always
     bring meaningful force to repel an invasion, regardless
     of empire size.
  2. Deep-defense bonus (when no in-region turret covers a
     tile) bumped from +0.4/turret cap +2.5 to +0.6/turret
     cap +5.0. So 9 turrets owned anywhere in your empire
     now means 6× cost-per-claim against snake attacks
     through interior regions, vs 3.5× before.

WHY: User reported the snake is STILL happening: "a unit will
live on my land with like 1 tile. When they expand, it's
almost like my defense unit and towers don't exist." Earlier
empire-wide deep-defense fix wasn't enough because the
underlying troop math gave the salient an asymmetric power
advantage. Big empires are no longer paper tigers in their
own territory.

---

[pending] gameplay: snake fix v3 — defender uses FULL empire pool

WHAT: Dropped the regional troop fraction entirely on the
defender side. Was `troops × fracD × morale × garrison × veterans`,
now just `troops × morale × garrison × veterans`. So a 200k-troop
empire defending its own land brings ALL 200k troops to bear,
not 30% (or 5% before that). Local modifiers — morale, garrison,
veterans, turret cost — still differentiate regional outcomes,
but the raw troop pool is empire-wide.

WHY: User pushed back on the 30% floor: "Why would I send 30%?
I should use my full power of that vassal to destroy the
encroaching enemy units." Right model — armies redeploy
quickly, and a 1-tile salient inside your empire shouldn't
face a curated subset of your forces. With this change a
small enemy salient inside a big empire now faces a brutal
ratio (their tiny attacker pool vs the defender's full pool)
and almost always loses.
