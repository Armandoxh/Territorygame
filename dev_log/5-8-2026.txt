[pending] gameplay: snake fix v2 — defender troop fraction floor
+ stronger deep-defense bonus

WHAT: Two changes to the regional combat math.
  1. Defender's regional troop fraction now floors at 0.30
     (was raw `regionTiles / empireTiles`). For a 2000-tile
     empire defending a 100-tile region, the old math gave 5%
     of the empire's troops as local defense — a 1-tile enemy
     salient could attack with FULL empire power vs 5% defender
     and grind unopposed. With a 30% floor, defenders always
     bring meaningful force to repel an invasion, regardless
     of empire size.
  2. Deep-defense bonus (when no in-region turret covers a
     tile) bumped from +0.4/turret cap +2.5 to +0.6/turret
     cap +5.0. So 9 turrets owned anywhere in your empire
     now means 6× cost-per-claim against snake attacks
     through interior regions, vs 3.5× before.

WHY: User reported the snake is STILL happening: "a unit will
live on my land with like 1 tile. When they expand, it's
almost like my defense unit and towers don't exist." Earlier
empire-wide deep-defense fix wasn't enough because the
underlying troop math gave the salient an asymmetric power
advantage. Big empires are no longer paper tigers in their
own territory.

  • industrial (T3 econ): per-tick (slow throttle), each owned
    settlement has 1% chance per stack to auto-bump a level.
  • watchtowers (T2 def): per-tick, players with the decree get

  • nuclear-program (T3 offense): each issue auto-fires a r12

  • forced-labor (T2 esp): tiles you capture pay 2× gold income

  • coup-detat (T3 esp): each issue flips one enemy region to

    enemy trade-route endpoint siphons 25% of that route's

    break — it just bleeds.
  • cold-war (T3 dipl): each issue shatters one alliance
    between two non-allied non-self players. Prefers an
    alliance involving someone you're at war with.

AI mastery picklists extended — ground AIs lean toward
watchtowers/industrial/forced-labor/coup-detat, air AIs grab
nuclear-program/cold-war, naval AIs prioritize privateer.

[pending] balance: trade rates uncap + scarcity pricing + gold trades + spread targets

WHAT: User: "If there's a scarce resource on the map why is the
offer capped at 15? Are AIs offering each other the same offers?
Make offers fairer/logical, no cap, and AIs should also offer
gold for resources."

Four changes:
  1. Removed the desperation cap (was clamped to 5 → max 15 res/s
     offer). Now uncapped — desperation = 1 + (200 - need) / 50,
     can grow significantly when AI is critically short.
  2. New _resScarcity(k): scans live-player totals for resource k,
     returns 1.0 (abundant, total >3000) up to 3.0 (rare, total
     <500). Pricing factors this in:
        ratio = scarcity(need) / scarcity(give)
        aGivesPerSec = base × desperation × ratio
     So an AI desperate for gems gives ×3 more food than an AI
     desperate for food gives gems. Matches real-world value.
  3. AI now proposes THREE trade types:
        • _aiProposeResForRes  (existing): res ↔ res
        • _aiProposeGoldForRes (NEW): gold → res, fires when
          AI has gold > 1200 and needs a resource
        • _aiProposeResForGold (NEW): res → gold, fires when
          AI has gold < 1000 and a stockpile of >300 of some res
  4. Target picker rewritten as weighted-random across all
     candidates with adequate supply, with a 0.5× downweight on
     human targets so AIs spread proposals across each other
     instead of all targeting player 1 (who has the biggest
     stockpile by default).

WHY: User's complaints were spot-on. The 15/s cap throttled
trading even when extreme imbalance warranted bigger offers; AIs
never proposed gold trades despite gold being a tradeable
currency; AI-to-AI trades were rare because the human kept
winning the "biggest supply" target selection.

[pending] gameplay: snake fix v5 — non-dominant defender dilution
+ connectivity gate kills 1-wide snakes

WHAT: User: "definitely a bug. The attack sends one pixel
straight up. If I cancel my attack on that vassal, the rogue
tile keeps expanding going 1 row at a time like the snake game."

Two compounding bugs identified:
  1. The salient owner DEFENDED with their full empire pool
     (v3 fix gave it to them). So a salient backed by a 200k-
     troop AI couldn't be cracked even with full player offense.
  2. Nothing structurally prevented a 1-tile or 1-wide-column
     salient from picking a cardinal direction and pushing
     into it tick after tick. Snake-game behavior.

Fixes:
  • Defender power now scales by region dominance. If defender
    is dominant in the contested region (>50% of region tiles),
    they bring their FULL empire pool — your home defense feels
    full-strength. If they're NOT dominant (e.g. a salient
    owner holding 1 of 100 tiles), they bring tile-projected
    force only with a 10% floor. So a 1-tile salient owner
    backed by a 200k-troop empire defends with ~20k power, not
    200k. Player attacker with full 100k crushes them.
  • New connectivity gate in _expand: AI / vassal frontier
    tiles need ≥ 2 same-owner cardinal neighbors to act as a
    push source. A 1-tile salient (0 same-owner neighbors)
    can't push at all. A 2-tile snake (each tile has 1) can't
    extend. Humans bypass the gate so peninsula tips still
    push when YOU drive them.

Combined effect: 1-tile salients are pinned in place AND
defend at 10%, so they wipe with even modest player offense.

[pending] gameplay: snake fix v4 — AI attacker projection scales
with regional foothold

WHAT: AI / vassal attackers (everyone except the human) now
have their attacker power scaled by how much of the target
region they already hold:
    fracA = clamp(ownTilesInTargetRegion / ownEmpire * 2, 0.10, 1)
    attackerPower *= fracA
The 10% floor keeps fresh pushes viable; the *2 lets a real
foothold scale up faster. Human attackers keep their full
empire troop pool — when you commit to an attack, your full
army goes.

WHY: User: "If the enemy has 1 tile on my property and I
surround them I should wipe them out with ease. Instead the
combination of 3x tap boost, offensive upgrades, and the use
of all my barracks activities can't wipe them out."

Root cause the previous v3 fix missed: when the snake-tile's
owner reclaimed lost territory, they too brought their FULL
empire troop pool through the salient. So a 1-tile salient
backed by a 200k-troop AI kept re-flipping every tile you
took back. Even barracks ops (which bypass defense math via
direct _claim) only covered tiles that immediately flipped
right back on the next tick.

With this fix: a 1-tile salient owner brings 10% of their
empire's troops to fight from there (the 1-tile foothold
floored). Defender (you) brings full empire pool. Salient's
attack power vs your defense: ~1:10 lopsided. They lose
every roll. Salient dies and stays dead.

[pending] gameplay: snake fix v3 — defender uses FULL empire pool

WHAT: Dropped the regional troop fraction entirely on the
defender side. Was `troops × fracD × morale × garrison × veterans`,
now just `troops × morale × garrison × veterans`. So a 200k-troop
empire defending its own land brings ALL 200k troops to bear,
not 30% (or 5% before that). Local modifiers — morale, garrison,
veterans, turret cost — still differentiate regional outcomes,
but the raw troop pool is empire-wide.

WHY: User pushed back on the 30% floor: "Why would I send 30%?
I should use my full power of that vassal to destroy the
encroaching enemy units." Right model — armies redeploy
quickly, and a 1-tile salient inside your empire shouldn't
face a curated subset of your forces. With this change a
small enemy salient inside a big empire now faces a brutal
ratio (their tiny attacker pool vs the defender's full pool)
and almost always loses.

[pending] gameplay: warship landings can no longer snake inland

WHAT: Warship CLUSTER LANDFALL only flips coastal tiles now.
A tile must be water-adjacent to be captured by naval
gunfire — primary AND any of the 4 cardinal adjacents.
Inland tiles still take 3x3 battery troop damage, but no
ownership flip. Target selection biased toward coastal enemy
tiles; inland tiles only get picked when no coast is in
range (the volley still drains troops there).

WHY: User report: "Im still seeing that snake bug, it looks
like its from when ships drop troops off on the shore. they
run through like a snake. i should be able to decimate them
too."

Root cause the v3/v4/v5 snake fixes missed: ship landings
go through tryCapture, not _expand. They bypass:
  - the connectivity gate (≥2 same-owner cardinal nbrs),
  - defender-dominance dilution (full empire pool when
    you're the dominant owner of the region),
  - attacker fracA scaling.
So every warship reload (~10 ticks) walked the bombardment
1 tile inland: primary flips, plus its 4 cardinal adjacents
flip. Even though _expand couldn't push the salient inland,
the warship kept planting fresh inland tiles on each volley.
That's the 1-tile-wide snake meandering through your land.

With this fix, naval guns are coastal weapons only.
Beachheads form along the shoreline (still snake-y along
the coast — that's beach-storming). To push inland the
attacker needs ground forces (barracks ground ops, vassal
expansion, infantry _expand) — all of which now have to
clear the v5 connectivity + dilution gates. Result: a
landed beachhead either gets reinforced into a real foothold
(spreads along the coast, builds a barracks, drops ground
ops) or it dies under the defender's full troop pool.
